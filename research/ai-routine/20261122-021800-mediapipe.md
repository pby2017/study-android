## Corrections and rationale

- Removed unsupported guaranteed 30–60 FPS, automatic CPU/GPU/NPU dispatch, and PyTorch-vs-MediaPipe 5-vs-60 FPS comparison. Current official overview says CPU/GPU acceleration and low-latency on-device execution, not a universal throughput guarantee or NPU auto-selection.
- Removed fixed hand/pose/face landmark counts from the general card because exact outputs depend on task/model/options and are unnecessary for the core Android lesson.
- Updated scope to the current MediaPipe Tasks API and documented Android IMAGE, VIDEO, and LIVE_STREAM modes.
- Added the real-time processing tradeoff: ordered synchronous VIDEO processing versus asynchronous LIVE_STREAM behavior that can drop incoming frames while busy to keep results current.
- Added device-level measurement across camera input, preprocessing, inference, and postprocessing rather than treating model-only throughput as app FPS.
