## Fact check and changes
- Reframed as a model-configuration / VRAM-budget lesson: LM plans song structure and DiT generates audio; VRAM capability depends on chosen model and offload strategy.
- Corrected the headline-level “<4GB VRAM” implication. The repository's actual recommendation for GPUs at or below 6GB is a 2B Turbo DiT, LM disabled, INT8 quantization, and full CPU offload.
- Kept the repository's A100 <2s and RTX 3090 <10s numbers only as author-reported figures, and stated that think mode/diffusion steps affect timing; no independent reproduction was performed.
- Replaced vague “few songs LoRA” with the repo's stated example: 8 songs, about 1 hour on RTX 3090 with 12GB VRAM.
- Verified Hugging Face model metadata lists MIT. Retained a reminder to review generated material because the project itself flags similarity/copyright risks; license metadata is not a blanket guarantee about output rights.
- Removed “commercial-grade/better than commercial” ranking and broad 16GB Mac/MLX claims from the card because they were unnecessary for the learning objective and were not independently substantiated in this pass.

## Decision
Retain as an AI developer workflow card, not an Android deployment claim. Teach hardware-tier configuration and empirical profiling rather than promote an unqualified benchmark or memory claim.

Confidence: high for the published configuration table, model composition, and license metadata; performance figures remain project-reported only.
