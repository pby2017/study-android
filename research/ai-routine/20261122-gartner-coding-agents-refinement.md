# Gartner AI coding-agent card refinement — 2026-11-22

## Decision
Replace the promotional “four leaders” framing with a reusable technology-selection method: understand what the quadrant measures, map it to team constraints and validate candidates on priority work through a proof of value.

## Sources checked
- https://www.gartner.com/en/documents/7879277 — Gartner's 2026 Magic Quadrant report abstract; title and publication date, 12 listed providers, and the two criteria (Ability to Execute and Completeness of Vision).
- https://www.gartner.com/en/documents/8338717 — Gartner's September 2026 selection-guide abstract says to select against expected outcomes, deployment constraints and priority use cases, then validate best-fit options through a proof of value.
- https://www.gartner.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-05-20-gartner-says-the-market-for-enterprise-ai-coding-agents-is-entering-a-new-phase-of-expansion-and-competitive-realignment — Gartner newsroom describes expansion from assisted development to agentic SDLC work and says governance, pricing, support, workflow, commercial maturity and durability matter in addition to developer experience and model capabilities.
- https://github.blog/ai-and-ml/github-copilot/github-recognized-as-a-leader-in-the-gartner-magic-quadrant-for-enterprise-ai-coding-agents-for-the-third-year-in-a-row/ — GitHub's vendor-authored summary corroborates the evaluated vendor count and quadrants; its own legal note says Gartner does not endorse vendors or recommend choosing only the highest-rated.

## Fact checks and corrections
- Gartner's official abstract lists 12 providers and identifies the report's two evaluation criteria.
- The previous card relied on a GitHub-authored blog to emphasize named quadrant leaders. Although the summary reflects the public chart, it is vendor-authored and gives readers little help in choosing a tool for their own Android team.
- Replaced “market movement means evaluate the whole SDLC” with the stronger sourcing distinction: Gartner describes the wider market, but its selection guidance specifically requires outcomes, constraints, priority use cases and PoV validation.
- Android evaluation examples (repository context, tests/review, access/audit and cost) are practical application suggestions, not claims attributed to Gartner.

## Card updates
- Added intermediate level, concise criteria, three key points, three primary Gartner sources, and an interview question/answer.
- Removed the “four leaders” and “2026 report renames assistants” headline angle to avoid implying a ranking is a purchase recommendation.

Confidence: high; report metadata and selection criteria come from Gartner's public abstracts and newsroom.
