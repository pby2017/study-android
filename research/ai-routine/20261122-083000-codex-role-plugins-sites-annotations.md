## Verified current state

The June 2 launch described six initial role-specific plugins and said the set included 62 apps and 110 skills. Those numbers are a launch-time snapshot, not a current catalog count. The launch described role-specific plugins as bundles of apps, skills, instructions, and workflows; today the Help Center describes plugins more generally as packages that can include connected apps, skills, app templates, and extensions.

An app-backed plugin does not grant access by itself. App availability, user authorization, role permissions, supported actions, and workspace controls continue to apply. Some plugins may be Codex-only or local until made available to a broader workspace.

Sites can create, preview, deploy, and share interactive websites and lightweight apps. Current help documentation lists paid plans and supported workspace configurations; availability depends on account, region, plan, and workspace role. A new Site is restricted to its owner and workspace admins by default. Sharing options depend on permissions, and public publishing requires the appropriate access; in Enterprise, an administrator must enable it. Building/editing a Site counts toward Codex usage, while hosting has separate/currently plan-specific treatment.

Annotations let users select a specific part of an output and request a localized change. The launch announcement said this extends beyond code, Markdown, and websites to documents, spreadsheets, and slides. This supports iterative review without asking the agent to redo all accepted content.

## Limitations

This is product documentation, not hands-on testing of plugin installs or Site publishing across account plans. Site and plugin availability can vary by rollout, region, role, workspace settings, and app permissions; recheck current help before adding plan-specific claims.
