- Category: AI
- Primary source: https://developers.googleblog.com/measuring-what-matters-with-jules/
- Supporting source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06717
- Exact claims verified this run:
  - Google Developers Blog publication date: 2026-06-22.
  - The eval frames proactive coding agents around insight policy, not only task completion.
  - Preliminary eval used 705 bugs and 1,178 CLs from internal Google codebases.
  - Agents explored up to three rounds before producing final insights.
  - LLM judge scores insight match from 1 to 5 and tracks Hit@K.
  - One exploration round averaged 4.5/5 on straightforward problems.
  - Hit@5 improved from 33% to 57% when exploration budget increased from two to three rounds.
